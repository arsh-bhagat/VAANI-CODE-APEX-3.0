Verified Legal Directives

Supreme Court Directive: A Zero FIR can be registered at any police station regardless of territorial jurisdiction where the incident occurred.

  • Police must assign temporary serial number "0" and transfer it to the competent precinct.
  • Duty officers must provide a free signed physical or digital copy of the FIR immediately.
  • Refusal to register an FIR for cognizable offences against women is punishable under Section 166A of IPC.
