## Brand & Style

The design system operates at the intersection of Bauhaus functionalism and Neo-Brutalist discipline, tailored specifically for high-stakes covert safety environments. The core brand ethos centers on tactical restraint: tools designed for extreme utility must avoid attention, minimize optical spill in low-light environments, and project architectural permanence.

The visual language rejects decorative neon glares, standard luminous white surfaces, and consumer-grade playful tropes. Instead, it employs flat geometric segmentation, utilitarian technical precision, and disciplined low-glow accents. The emotional resonance is calm, unyielding, and covert—instilling absolute confidence without drawing physical attention to the screen. Visual hierarchy is achieved through proportion, technical grid-alignment, and disciplined tonal value rather than glowing illumination.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for low-lux discretion, optical density, and zero pure-white emissions.

### Surface Tones
- **Base Canvas (`#0d0d0f` to `#121214`):** The structural foundation. Absorbs ambient light and conceals screen edges in darkness.
- **Card Panel Default (`#1a1a1d`):** Primary tier for grouping operational data. Flat, authoritative, unadorned.
- **Card Panel Elevated (`#212124`):** Interactive containers, modals, and raised telemetry panels.
- **Structural Borders (`#2a2a2e` to `#3a3a40`):** Precise 1px to 1.5px partitions providing structural definition without high-contrast friction.

### Accents & Operational Signals
- **Primary / Armed Active (`#c9a227` / `#d4aa30`):** Muted, desaturated industrial gold/amber. Signals primed states, armed triggers, and confirmed operational status without blinding the user.
- **Alert / SOS Destructive (`#8f2d2d` / `#9e3232`):** Serious, desaturated crimson. Reserved exclusively for emergency broadcast, distress engagement, and destructive commands.
- **Interactive / Mesh / Info (`#3d6b7d` / `#4a6fa5`):** Muted steel blue and slate teal. Handles mesh network diagnostics, routing indicators, and secondary controls.

### Typography Shades
- **Primary Bone (`#e8e6e0`):** High-legibility off-white with warm mineral undertones. Prevents retinal fatigue and screen bleed.
- **Secondary Mid-Gray (`#9a9a9e`):** Metadata, axis labels, and inactive control parameters.
- **Muted Carbon (`#5a5a60`):** Grid markings, terminal bracket lines, and dormant status indicators.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines geometric Bauhaus construction with technical instrumentation legibility.

- **Primary Display & Interface Font:** `Space Grotesk`. Delivers idiosyncratic geometric brutalism with deep ink-traps and wide apertures. Headings must be set tightly with negative letter spacing to preserve an architectural silhouette.
- **Telemetry & Systems Font:** `JetBrains Mono`. Dedicated to hardware telemetry, coordinates, status indicators, payload timestamps, and micro-metrics. Uppercase transformation is required for system tags, accompanied by expanded letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to guarantee legibility at minimal sizes.

Numbers in UI panels should consistently use tabular figures to ensure aligned data columns across real-time feeds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a rigid modular grid that prioritizes tactical scanning and muscle-memory touch targets.

### Grid Configuration
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `0.75rem` (12px) gutter, `1rem` (16px) canvas margin. Critical controls and triggers span all 4 columns for reliable single-hand activation.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `1rem` (16px) gutter, `1.5rem` (24px) canvas margin.
- **Desktop / Terminal Dashboard (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed or fluid container, capped at 1440px width with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Structural side rails lock system telemetry to persistent visual coordinates.

### Spacing Rhythm
Empty space functions as an active structural divider rather than passive padding. Layouts are partitioned into hard rectangular zones. Internal component padding follows a strict 4px base increment (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Vertical density is kept compact to minimize the need for scrolling during high-stress operations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses flat tonal layering and precise geometric boundaries, completely avoiding ambient drop shadows, blurred light sources, and glowing blooms that compromise screen covertness.

1. **Surface Step Architecture:** Depth is defined strictly through value stepping.
   - Level 0 (Base canvas): `#0d0d0f`
   - Level 1 (Structural panels, lists): `#1a1a1d`
   - Level 2 (Floating panels, overlays, drawers): `#212124`
   - Level 3 (Active interactive surfaces): `#28282d`

2. **Border Reinforcement:** Surfaces are separated by 1px or 1.5px solid strokes (`#2a2a2e`). When a component is focused, armed, or hovered, the stroke shifts cleanly to `#3a3a40` or the respective functional accent (`#c9a227`, `#8f2d2d`, `#3d6b7d`) without size expansion.

3. **Restrained Low-Glow Rule:** Glows are never used for atmospheric styling. In instances of active armed states or distress broadcasting, an element may use a strict 1px inset or exterior keyline with zero blur radius, keeping the light strictly bounded by the element's footprint.

## Shapes

The design system uses a tight 4px border radius (`roundedness: 1`).

This subtle corner curvature softens harsh micro-pixel collisions on dense screens while preserving the discipline of Bauhaus geometry. Pure circular pills are strictly reserved for compact status badges, operational chips, and telemetry indicators to set them apart from structural cards and command buttons. Structural containers, panels, inputs, and primary action blocks consistently maintain the 4px curvature.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary / Armed Trigger:** Deep charcoal base (`#1a1a1d`) framed by a 1.5px solid amber border (`#c9a227`). Typography in crisp bone (`#e8e6e0`), `Space Grotesk` Medium, with an uppercase `JetBrains Mono` status sub-label. On press/active: Background shifts to `#c9a227` with contrasting dark text (`#0d0d0f`).
- **Destructive / SOS Action:** Deep charcoal base (`#1a1a1d`) with a 1.5px crimson outline (`#8f2d2d`). Text rendered in bone (`#e8e6e0`). Press state fills background with crimson (`#8f2d2d`).
- **Secondary / Tactical Neutral:** Background `#212124`, 1px border `#2a2a2e`, text `#9a9a9e`. Active state brightens text to `#e8e6e0` and border to `#3a3a40`.

### Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** Solid `#1a1a1d` background, 4px corner radius, 1px border `#2a2a2e`.
- **Card Header:** Separated from body via a 1px continuous border line (`#2a2a2e`). Header displays category in uppercase `JetBrains Mono` (`label-md`) with `#9a9a9e` color.
- **Interactive State:** Hover or focus transitions the 1px border to `#3a3a40` with zero elevation lift or translation on the Y-axis.

### Status Badges (Pills)
- **Geometry:** Height 22px, full pill radius (9999px), inline padding 8px.
- **Armed / Online:** Deep tinted gold background (`#282210`), border 1px `#c9a227` at 50% opacity, label in `#d4aa30` (`JetBrains Mono`, 10px uppercase).
- **Critical / SOS:** Deep tinted red background (`#2a1212`), border 1px `#8f2d2d` at 60% opacity, label in `#e57373`.
- **Mesh / Node Active:** Deep slate teal background (`#122026`), border 1px `#3d6b7d` at 50% opacity, label in `#7aa2b2`.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Rectangular, `#121214` background with a inset 1px border `#2a2a2e`, 4px radius.
- **Typography:** `JetBrains Mono` 13px in `#e8e6e0`. Placeholder in `#5a5a60`.
- **Focus:** 1px crisp outline in `#3d6b7d` or `#c9a227`. Zero glow, drop-shadow, or blur rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Form:** 16x16px square (checkbox) or 16x16px circle (radio).
- **Idle:** Solid `#121214` with a 1px border `#3a3a40`.
- **Checked:** Solid `#1a1a1d` base with a centered 8x8px geometric square or circle mark filled with `#c9a227`. 1px border `#c9a227`.

### Lists & Telemetry Feeds
- **Row Configuration:** Edge-to-edge `#1a1a1d` rows divided by a 1px hairline `#2a2a2e`. Zero inter-row vertical margin.
- **Data Columns:** Left-aligned entity identifier in `Space Grotesk` (`body-md`), right-aligned tabular timestamp/coordinates in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-md`) with `#9a9a9e` color.

### Hardware Killswitch / Slider Toggle
- **Track:** Recessed `#0d0d0f`, 1px solid `#2a2a2e`, height 28px, 4px radius.
- **Thumb:** Flat `#212124` block with a 1px border `#3a3a40`, height 24px, width 36px, 2px radius. Active engagement transitions thumb border to `#c9a227`.
